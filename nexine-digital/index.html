FAQ

Questions we hear often

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we reply within working hours.

How long does a website take?

A Starter site usually goes live in 7 working days. Business sites take 2–3 weeks and online stores 3–5 weeks, depending on how quickly content and photos arrive.

Do I need to buy the domain and hosting myself?

No. We can register the domain and set up hosting in your name, so you always own them. If you already have them, we work with what you have.

Can I update the website myself later?

Yes. We build on easy editors such as WordPress and give you a short training session and a video guide.

Can the website be in Bengali?

Yes. We build bilingual English/বাংলা sites with a language switch, and AI assistants that reply in Bengali, Hindi or English.

How do payments work?

Usually 50% to start and 50% before launch, by UPI or bank transfer. Larger projects can be split into milestones.
