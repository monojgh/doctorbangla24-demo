FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to stop eating rice?

No. Most plans keep rice and roti in the right portions. We change quantities and combinations, not your whole diet.

Can I join only yoga or Zumba?

Yes. You can join classes on their own or combine them with a diet plan for faster results.

I have diabetes and take medicine. Is this safe?

Your diet plan works alongside your doctor's treatment. Please keep taking your medicines and bring recent reports to the first consultation.

Do you offer online consultation?

Call or WhatsApp us to ask about phone or video consultations and follow-ups.
